Privacy & cookies.

SecretShare is designed to know as little as possible. This policy explains what the current website processes, why it does so, and the controls available to you.

Effective: 24 August 2026

Overview

This policy applies to the SecretShare website. The current public site is a front-end demonstration: it does not transmit, store, or deliver the secret text or files entered into the interface.

If the service later adds accounts, a backend, analytics, payments, or production secret delivery, this policy will be updated before those features process personal data.

Data we process

We do not ask you to create an account on this website. When you visit, infrastructure providers may automatically process technical information required to deliver and secure the page, such as your IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamp, and basic request logs.

If you contact us, we process the information in your message so we can respond.

Secret text and files

In the current demonstration, content entered into the composer remains in your browser. Clicking “Seal and create link” creates a visual example only; it does not upload the content or create a working share link.

Do not rely on this demonstration to deliver real credentials or confidential files.

Cookies and local storage

We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. The site stores one preference named privacy-lock-cookie-choice in your browser’s local storage after you accept or decline the banner. It exists only to remember that choice and does not identify you across websites.

You can clear it in your browser settings or use this control: .

Blocking local storage does not prevent you from using the site, though the notice may reappear.

Advertising status

SecretShare does not currently load Google AdSense, request advertisements, set advertising cookies, or have a Google publisher ID. The development preference banner is not an advertising consent platform.

Before advertising is enabled, this policy will identify Google and applicable advertising providers, explain their use of cookies, local storage, IP addresses and other identifiers, and provide the controls required for the visitor’s region. Traffic from the EEA, UK, and Switzerland will use a Google-certified consent management platform where required. Advertising code will not be placed on the secret composer, recipient view, legal pages, or other non-editorial screens.

Service providers and transfers

  • Development hosting may change before launch. When GitHub Pages is used, GitHub may process request logs under its own privacy terms.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site’s typefaces. Your browser may connect to Google and disclose technical request information, including your IP address.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Retention and security

The cookie preference stays on your device until you clear browser data or reset it. Infrastructure logs are retained according to the relevant hosting provider’s operational and security policies. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and meet legitimate legal or security requirements.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but we aim to minimise collection and limit access to information that is genuinely needed.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Because this site does not have user accounts, we may need enough information to verify and locate a relevant contact message or record before fulfilling a request.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@privacylock.io. If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated and a prominent notice will be posted when appropriate.
